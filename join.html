Why Join Us?

As a group, we want to help create a single payer system for every resident of the United States to have healthcare. We believe that healthcare should have no cost and should be available to everyone, no matter their income level; access healthcare should be a right, not a privilege. With a single payer system, people who already struggle to find money for everyday needs could have the weight of rising healthcare costs lifted off their shoulders.
