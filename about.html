About Us

Our Group

We are a group of dedicated, and active citizens who are passionate about extending government benefits to more Americans. We are trying to solve the problem of healthcare, so that people can focus on actual economic issues rather than worrying about their healthcare.

Our Goals

  • Lower the average cost of healthcare for Americans to be less than 100$ a year
  • Make America run a government funded health care system in which the average American can get their health covered by government funding
  • Ensure that every single American has access to healthcare
